Ordered Engineering Diagnostics

The 14-Rung SEO Diagnostic Ladder

Fixing content depth on a page blocked by robots.txt is wasted effort. The ladder enforces fixing prerequisites in the exact order search engines process them.

01
Host Reachable & TLS

DNS resolution, HTTPS certificate valid, redirect chains from HTTP/non-www.

02
Robots.txt & Crawl Blocks

No unintentional disallow rules blocking Googlebot from CSS, JS, or money pages.

03
Canonical Integrity

Self-referential, cross-domain, or parameterized canonicals verified without loops.

04
Indexability & Headers

X-Robots-Tag, noindex meta tags, and 404/301 status hygiene.

05
Schema & Structured Data

Valid JSON-LD markup without syntax errors for articles, products, and FAQs.

06
Response Status & Redirects

Eliminates 301 redirect chains, 5xx server drops, and soft 404 dead ends.

07
Content Quality & Thin Pages

Identifies duplicate content, thin bodies (<300 words), and boilerplate bloat.

08
Heading & Title Architecture

Strict H1 uniqueness, missing meta descriptions, and truncated title tags.

09
Internal Link Flow & Orphans

Finds unlinked orphan pages and optimizes PageRank flow to conversion pages.

10
Real-User Core Web Vitals

Audits 75th-percentile real-world LCP, INP, and CLS against Google's thresholds.

11
Mobile & Viewport Usability

Validates mobile-first indexing, responsive viewport tags, and touch targets.

12
Security & Response Headers

HSTS, CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, and Referrer-Policy audit scores.

13
Social Metadata & Open Graph

Verifies og:image, twitter:card, and social preview fallbacks for viral sharing.

14
Hreflang & Internationalization

Validates reciprocal language alternates and prevents regional indexing penalties.

Intent match — stage 3

The one stage nothing can infer from your page alone. Tell it the query a page targets and it reads the live SERP in your Chrome, then compares what Google rewards against what your page is.

Keyword research

This runs before everything else. Without a keyword-to-URL map, "intent mismatch", "cannibalisation" and "content gap" are assertions rather than findings.

What the free tier can't give you. No search volume — nothing free returns it. Breadth counts how many independent autocomplete probes surfaced a phrase, which is a popularity hint, not volume. There's no live SERP scrape either, so clustering by SERP similarity — the correct method — isn't available here. Cluster by tokens as a first pass, then sample real SERPs before committing a page to a cluster. With Search Console connected, the landing-page clustering in that section is the stronger proxy: it's Google's own choice of page.

Search Console

The difference between observed and inferred. Without this connected the tool infers indexation from a crawl; with it, you read what Google actually decided.

Two ways in. The quick one needs no setup: in Search Console open Performance, set your date range, click Export → Download CSV, unzip it, and drop the files below. That is Google's own data with no OAuth, no Cloud project, no keys. The OAuth route below adds live refresh and URL Inspection if you want them later.

Plan a new site

There is nothing to audit before a site exists, so the diagnostic half of this tool does not apply yet. What replaces it is the set of decisions that are cheap now and expensive after launch — most audit work on established sites is undoing choices someone made in week one.

People

Who owns which finding, and how much is on each of them. Work with no owner does not ship — that's the most common reason an audit produces no change.

This is assignment, not login. The tool runs on your own machine with no TLS, so a password screen here would protect nothing — anyone with the machine has the data files regardless. Rather than imply a guarantee that isn't there, this tracks ownership and load, which is the part that actually affects whether work gets done.

Behaviour

What people actually did on the page. A rage-click cluster or a dead click explains a conversion problem that rankings and Core Web Vitals never will.

Competitors

Crawl a competitor and compare how the two sites are built. Their HTML is public, so this needs no subscription and no API.

What this can and cannot tell you. It compares architecture, schema, content depth, internal linking and trust signals — the parts you can copy or beat. It cannot tell you their traffic, backlinks or rankings; those need a clickstream panel and a web-scale link graph, which is what an Ahrefs or Semrush subscription actually buys.

Server logs

Your server's own record of what Googlebot actually did. A crawl proves a page is reachable; only the log proves Google bothered to fetch it.

Nothing leaves your machine. The file is parsed in this process and never stored. Combined (Apache/nginx), IIS W3C, and JSON lines from a CDN are all detected automatically.

Where to find it: cPanel → Raw Access Logs; WP Engine → Logs; Cloudflare → Logpush; nginx → /var/log/nginx/access.log. A week is plenty; a month is better. Crawl the site first — the comparison between what Google requested and what exists is where most of the value is.
